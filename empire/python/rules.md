# Spielregeln Total War: EMPIRE (für den Feldherrn)

Nur die Spiegelstriche gehen an das Modell. Fakten über das Spiel und harte Regeln des Kommandos.
Ergänzen/korrigieren erwünscht – Erfahrungen aus Schlachten-Logs hier eintragen.

- Line infantry fires volleys at about 70-90 m (see each unit's "range"); hit chance and casualties rise sharply as the distance shrinks. Out of range they cannot hurt anyone.
- Light infantry and riflemen reach further (100-150 m) and are accurate but weak in melee; they should not stand in the open against cavalry.
- Artillery outranges everything (400-1000 m). It is deadly against massed infantry and helpless when cavalry or infantry reach it; it cannot move while deployed and turns slowly.
- Cavalry charging into the flank or rear of infantry or artillery routs it quickly; charging the front of formed line infantry that is firing usually fails with heavy losses.
- Units that lose many men in a short time, are hit in the flank or rear, or see the general die or nearby units rout, may break and run (ROUTING). Routing units do not obey orders.
- Running tires units; tired units fire and fight worse. Walk to positions, run only to charge or to escape.
- A unit in melee cannot fire. Infantry fighting hand to hand is pinned and easy to flank.
- Never order units of the enemy army - only your own unit ids are executed.
- The general's bodyguard keeps nearby units steady; if he dies, the whole army's morale suffers. Keep him behind the line, out of musket range.
- Units that move face the way they march; to face the enemy, give the final move a facing or move towards the enemy.
