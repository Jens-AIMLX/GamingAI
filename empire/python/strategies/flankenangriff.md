# Flankenangriff – Front binden, Flanke umfassen

Nur die Spiegelstriche gehen an das Modell.

- Pin the enemy front with a line of infantry and the artillery; it holds at 100-120 m and fires, without closing in.
- Send a strong flanking force (cavalry and some light or line infantry) on a wide arc round the weaker enemy wing, using waypoints out of his musket range.
- Time the flank attack: the flanking force waits at its starting point until it is in place, then strikes the flank or rear while the front attacks at the same moment.
- Watch for his reaction: if he turns units to face the flank, the front line advances and hits them while they turn.
- Keep a small reserve to cover your own exposed flank against his cavalry.
