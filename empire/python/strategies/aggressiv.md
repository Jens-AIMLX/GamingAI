# Aggressiv – Initiative und Entscheidung suchen

Nur die Spiegelstriche gehen an das Modell.

- Seize the initiative: advance early in line, before the enemy has settled, with artillery moving up behind the infantry.
- Concentrate on one part of the enemy line: bring most of your infantry against one wing or the centre and break it, while a few units only pin the rest.
- Close to 60-70 m before firing; a few heavy volleys at short range followed by a bayonet charge (run=true) break wavering units.
- Use cavalry boldly but not stupidly: hit the flank or rear of units already engaged with your infantry, ride down artillery, chase routers.
- Do not stop to trade volleys at long range for minutes; time works against the attacker.
- Once an enemy unit breaks, immediately turn the units that broke it on the next one.
