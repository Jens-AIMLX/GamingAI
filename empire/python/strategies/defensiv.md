# Defensiv – halten und abnutzen

Nur die Spiegelstriche gehen an das Modell.

- Choose a strong position early (high ground, a wood or village on a flank) and form one closed line there; do not leave it unless the enemy refuses to attack until the time runs short.
- Let the enemy come to you: hold fire discipline, keep the line formed and let his units walk into your volleys.
- Keep a reserve of one or two infantry units behind the centre and plug any gap the moment a unit wavers.
- Artillery fires at whatever comes closest; keep it behind the line with keep_range so cavalry cannot reach it.
- Cavalry stays behind the flanks with engage_within_m 80-120: it only strikes what comes round a flank or what is already broken.
- Counter-attack only units that are already routing or badly weakened, and return to the line afterwards.
- If the enemy will not attack, use your artillery and light infantry to make standing still costly for him.
